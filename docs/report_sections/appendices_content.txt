<h2>APPENDIX B: USER MANUAL AND OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS</h2>
<p class=MsoNormal style="text-indent:0.5in;">
This user manual provides step-by-step operating guidelines for student applicants and departmental administrators using the IntraWorld platform.
</p>

<h3>B.1 STUDENT ONBOARDING &amp; VERIFICATION GUIDE</h3>
<p class=MsoNormal>
<strong>Step 1: Accessing the Portal</strong><br/>
Navigate to the platform root URL (e.g., <code>https://intraworld.edu</code> or local test host) in any modern, standards-compliant web browser. On the landing page, click the <strong>Register Account</strong> button to launch the verification wizard.
</p>
<p class=MsoNormal>
<strong>Step 2: Entering Institutional Affiliation</strong><br/>
Provide your full legal name, institutional email address (e.g., Gmail), mobile phone number, and type your college name (e.g., &ldquo;Seshadripuram First Grade College&rdquo;).
</p>
<p class=MsoNormal>
<strong>Step 3: Uploading the Physical Student ID Card</strong><br/>
Click the <strong>Upload Student ID Card</strong> upload tile. Select a clear, high-resolution photograph of your physical college identity card. Ensure proper lighting, avoid glare on the text lines, and keep the card flat against a neutral surface.
</p>
<p class=MsoNormal>
<strong>Step 4: Automated Vision Scanning &amp; Credential Locking</strong><br/>
The client-side vision pipeline automatically normalizes the image, reads matrix barcodes (if present), and executes neural OCR via WebAssembly. The system cross-evaluates your college name against the card text. Upon successful validation, your <strong>Degree Course</strong> and <strong>Roll Number</strong> will populate automatically and lock with a green padlock indicator.
</p>
<p class=MsoNormal>
<strong>Step 5: Two-Factor Authentication Verification</strong><br/>
Click <strong>Verify Email OTP</strong>. A 6-digit cryptographic verification code will be dispatched to your email address. Enter the code in the verification modal. Repeat the step for SMS mobile verification (or use Demo OTP mode during evaluation trials).
</p>
<p class=MsoNormal>
<strong>Step 6: Submission and Pending Queue</strong><br/>
Complete the interactive Cloudflare Turnstile challenge and click <strong>Submit Registration</strong>. Your application is placed into the secure departmental verification queue with status <code>pending</code>.
</p>

<h3>B.2 COLLEGE ADMINISTRATOR CONSOLE GUIDE</h3>
<p class=MsoNormal>
<strong>Step 1: Logging into Administrative Console</strong><br/>
Open <code>admin_approval.html</code> and authenticate using authorized faculty credentials.
</p>
<p class=MsoNormal>
<strong>Step 2: Reviewing the Verification Queue</strong><br/>
The dashboard displays pending applicants in real time. Each record presents the student's name, roll number, course, calculated Authenticity Trust Score, and a thumbnail of the uploaded ID card.
</p>
<p class=MsoNormal>
<strong>Step 3: Inspecting Proofs and Authorizing Accounts</strong><br/>
Click any applicant to open the split-screen inspection view. Compare the entered roll number against the high-resolution photo on the left panel. If authentic, click <strong>Approve Account</strong> to commit the student to the active roster and trigger a welcome email. If the card is fraudulent or unreadable, click <strong>Reject Application</strong> and provide a reason.
</p>
