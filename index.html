Explore IntraWorld

Intra World is proposed as a dedicated digital networking platform for UG and PG students. Its primary purpose is to create a trusted student community where users can connect based on their academic requirements and professional interests. Students require a more focused environment where they can build meaningful academic connections and discover educational and career opportunities without unnecessary entertainmentoriented content.
